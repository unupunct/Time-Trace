TimeTrace - privacy summary
===========================

TimeTrace measures how long each application is in the foreground on this
computer and shows it as timelines and reports.

What it records
  - the active application (name, executable, path) and its window title
    (window titles can be switched off)
  - periods when you were idle (no keyboard or mouse input)

What it never does
  - no keystrokes, no mouse movement, no screenshots, no clipboard contents,
    no microphone, no webcam
  - no account, no cloud synchronisation, no telemetry, no uploads

Your data
  - stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\TimeTrace
  - you can pause or stop tracking at any time, exclude applications and
    window titles, export and delete everything

This installer does not start tracking and does not add TimeTrace to Windows
startup. TimeTrace asks for your consent the first time it runs.
